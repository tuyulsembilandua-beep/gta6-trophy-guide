    Where every fact comes from

    Each trophy's guide names its sources beside it. The two sources below cover the whole list; the guides themselves are our own words, written from the official descriptions — never copied from another guide.

      What we do — and do not — claim

      • Official data is exact. Names, descriptions and grades are PlayStation's list as published on Exophase and cross-checked on PSNProfiles on 18 Nov 2026. The counts PSN tracks (42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone race locations, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 8 garages) come from PSNProfiles.
      • Guides are our reading of the descriptions. Where a description names a mission, a count or an activity, the guide says how to approach it. Where no location has been verified, the guide says so instead of inventing one.
      • Missable flags are a judgement call, labelled as one. A description that names a single mission or moment with no replay named gets flagged; chapter trophies do not.
      • Hidden trophies stay hidden. 28 of the 55 are hidden on PlayStation until earned. Turn on “Hide story spoilers” and their names and descriptions stay hidden here until you earn them.

      Your progress stays yours

      Checklist progress is saved in this browser's localStorage and nowhere else. Export writes a JSON file you can keep or move to another browser; Import reads it back. The app makes no network request at run time — every file it loads is one of its own.
